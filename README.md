# Diagnostic-Pathology-Test-Results-Analysis


## Project Overview

The Diagnostic Pathology Test Results Analysis is a healthcare data analytics project focused on exploring patients' diagnostic test results and identifying patterns in important medical biomarkers across different health conditions.

The dataset was designed to support healthcare research, disease-risk assessment, and machine learning classification by using nine key medical indicators to classify individuals into five health condition categories: Fit, Anaemia, Hypertension, Diabetes, and High Cholesterol.

For this project, I used Microsoft Power BI to perform descriptive analysis and exploratory data analysis (EDA), transforming the diagnostic dataset into an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard provides insights into patient distribution, average medical measurements, comparisons across health conditions, and relationships between selected diagnostic indicators.

## Project Objectives

The major objectives of this analysis were to:

- Examine the distribution of patients across different health conditions.
- Analyse average blood glucose and HbA1c levels across conditions.
- Evaluate blood pressure patterns using systolic and diastolic measurements.
- Compare haemoglobin and MCV levels across patient conditions.
- Examine HDL and LDL cholesterol levels across different conditions.
- Identify patterns and relationships between selected diagnostic indicators.
- Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset Description

**Dataset Name:** `health_markers_dataset`

The dataset contains patient diagnostic information and health-related measurements used for examining different medical conditions.

The analysis covers the following health indicators:

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| Condition | Health condition assigned to each patient |
| Blood_glucose | Blood glucose measurement |
| HbA1C | Indicator of average blood glucose over approximately 2–3 months |
| Systolic_BP | Blood pressure recorded during heart contraction |
| Diastolic_BP | Blood pressure recorded during heart relaxation |
| Haemoglobin | Measurement of haemoglobin concentration |
| MCV | Mean Corpuscular Volume |
| HDL | High-Density Lipoprotein cholesterol |
| LDL | Low-Density Lipoprotein cholesterol |
| Triglycerides | Type of fat measured in the blood |

## Analytical Process

The project followed these major stages:

### Step 1: Data Importation
The health markers dataset was imported into Power BI for analysis.

### Step 2: Data Preparation
The dataset was prepared for analysis by reviewing the available fields and ensuring that the relevant variables could be used for calculations and visualisations.

### Step 3: Data Analysis
The diagnostic indicators were examined using average values and patient counts to understand differences across the recorded conditions.

### Step 4: KPI Development
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) were created to provide a summary of important health measurements and the total patient population.

### Step 5: Data Visualisation
Different charts were used to communicate the findings, compare health indicators, and examine relationships between selected variables.

### Step 6: Dashboard Development
An interactive dashboard was designed with a condition slicer, allowing users to filter the analysis and explore the results for individual conditions.

## Key Findings

Based on the dashboard:

1. **Patient Distribution:** The Fit category accounts for approximately 72% of the patient population, representing the largest group in the dataset.

2. **Diabetes:** Diabetes accounts for approximately 12.21% of the patients, making it the second-largest category.

3. **Hypertension:** Hypertension represents approximately 7.03% of the patient population.

4. **High Cholesterol:** Approximately 5.29% of the patients fall within the high-cholesterol category.

5. **Overall Blood Glucose:** The average blood glucose measurement across the dataset is 102.77.

6. **Blood Pressure:** The overall average systolic and diastolic blood pressure measurements are 112.78 and 72.12, respectively.

7. **Haemoglobin:** The overall average haemoglobin measurement is 13.81, while the average MCV is 87.34.

8. **Cholesterol:** The overall average HDL and LDL measurements are 48.91 and 100.33, respectively.
